About Us

Meet Kermit

Kermit's Purrs was born from a simple idea: the rumble of a happy cat is one of nature's most powerful stress relievers. We built an app that captures that magic and puts it in your pocket — perfect for winding down, focusing, or just feeling a little less alone.


Whether you're a seasoned cat person or just need a moment of calm, Kermit's got you covered with hours of authentic, high-quality purr recordings.
